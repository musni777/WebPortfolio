FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our portfolio website services.

How long does it take to build my website?

▼

All packages are delivered within 2–3 days from when you send your details. Premium package orders get priority treatment. We work quickly so you can start sharing your portfolio as soon as possible.

Do I need to buy domain and hosting?

▼

We will guide you step-by-step to buy a domain name and set up hosting. You don't need any technical knowledge — we walk you through the whole process. Domain and hosting costs are separate from our package prices.

Can I request changes to my website?

▼

Yes! Revisions are included in every package. After we send you the preview, you can request changes and we'll update the design until you're happy. Additional revision rounds beyond the included ones are available as an add-on.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

▼

Yes, absolutely. All websites we build are 100% mobile responsive — they look great and work perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. This is included in every package at no extra cost.

How do I send my details to get started?

▼

Simply send your CV, a photo, your skills list, and any other content you want on the website via email (xthatxthat@gmail.com) or WhatsApp (+94 706 578 348). We'll take care of the rest!

What is included in each package?

▼

Basic (500 LKR): 1–3 pages, About, Skills, Contact form. Professional (750 LKR): Up to 5 pages, Resume/CV section, Project showcase. Premium (1000 LKR): Up to 7 pages, custom design, LinkedIn integration. All include mobile responsive design and 2–3 day delivery.

How do I pay for my website?

▼

Contact us via WhatsApp or email and we'll discuss payment options that work for you. We'll provide payment details once you confirm your package.

Will prices stay the same after the launch offer?

▼

No — these are special launch prices for the first 3 clients only. After 3 orders, prices will increase. We recommend contacting us now to lock in these discounted rates.
